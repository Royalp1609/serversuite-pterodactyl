# Code of conduct

Server Suite is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part (in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is
represented) is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to Pterodactyl,
  Minecraft servers or open source.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and that the reason may be
  scope or time rather than quality.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information (addresses, IPs, emails,
  real names) without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Spam, advertising, or selling access to mods, modpacks or game versions
  through this project.

## Reporting

Email **royalp1609@protonmail.com** with links or screenshots. Reports are
read only by the maintainer and kept confidential. Security problems go
through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and
block people from the repository, temporarily or permanently, depending on
what happened. Clear-cut abuse can be removed without warning.

This policy is based on the ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
